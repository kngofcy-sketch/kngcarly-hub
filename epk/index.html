Comunicado Oficial: "TINTA Y HONOR REMIX"

KNG_CARLY REVEALS RAW TESTAMENT TO RESILIENCE WITH NEW LATIN HIP-HOP ANTHEM "TINTA Y HONOR REMIX"

Release Date: September 18, 2026 | Distribution: Soundbirth | Label: Independent

Bridging the gap between the gritty streets of Havana and the neon-lit hustle of Miami, KNG_CARLY returns to the forefront of the Latin Hip-Hop scene with the release of "TINTA Y HONOR REMIX." Scheduled for global release on September 18, 2026, the track is a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling, blending hard-hitting Latin Drill with the dark, cinematic textures of modern urban street anthems.

"TINTA Y HONOR REMIX" serves as a sonic manifesto for loyalty and personal evolution. The track explores the unwritten codes of the independent grind, translating the weight of sacrifice into a high-octane musical experience. Built upon heavy 808 glides and aggressive sliding basslines, the production creates an ominous yet uplifting backdrop for KNG_CARLY’s sharp, unfiltered vocal delivery. It is an introspective journey that refuses to shy away from the scars earned while building an empire on one’s own terms.

The track’s inspiration draws heavily from the evolution of Latin Trap, favoring a mood that is both brooding and triumphant. By eschewing mainstream polish for raw authenticity, KNG_CARLY captures the essence of a diaspora artist navigating the complexities of street loyalty and creative sovereignty. The "Remix" is not merely a new version of a song, but a reinforced statement of intent for an artist who has remained fiercely independent throughout his career.

"This track is about the ink on the paper and the honor in the blood," says KNG_CARLY. "It represents the silent sacrifices and the principles that don’t bend, no matter how much the environment changes. Coming from Cuba to the Miami circuit, I’ve learned that your word and your work are all you truly own. 'Tinta y Honor' is for everyone who is staying true to their roots while they build their own kingdom."

Distributed globally via Soundbirth, "TINTA Y HONOR REMIX" marks a pivotal chapter in KNG_CARLY’s discography. The release also serves as a precursor to exclusive material and upcoming drops designated for The Kingdom Pass community, further cementing the artist’s commitment to his core audience and his burgeoning independent legacy.

01. Pitch de Prensa & Biografía

KNG_CARLY es un artista y productor independiente cubano radicado en Hialeah, Miami, que redefine el Latin Trap y Urban Drill mediante una fórmula implacable: 808s pesados, baterías cortantes y una narrativa cruda basada en disciplina, superación y realidad de calle, influenciada por la densidad lírica de autores como Melendi o Ricardo Arjona.

Tras irrumpir con fuerza orgánica gracias al impacto de "Niveles" —respaldado directamente por curadores y su distribuidora Soundbirth— y viralizar contenido que supera las 220,000 visualizaciones en redes, KNG_CARLY consolida una propuesta independiente con estándar de grandes ligas, encabezada por sus estrenos oficiales: "TINTA Y HONOR REMIX" (18 de Septiembre) y "El Ungido".
